Privacy Policy
MechRef

Last updated: November 21, 2026  ·  Publisher: HRSoftware

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how HRSoftware ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the MechRef mobile and desktop application ("the App").

MechRef's engineering content runs entirely on-device and requires no account. The App collects no data at all — nothing is sent to us, and nothing is shared with third parties. The advertising SDK present in earlier versions was removed in version 1.0.7.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

· Personal identification information (name, email, address, phone number)
· Location data
· Contacts, messages, photos, or files
· Browser or search history
· Crash logs or diagnostics

· Advertising identifiers or app-event data
· Analytics of any kind

This applies to both the Android and the Windows versions.

In-App Purchases

MechRef offers optional in-app purchases to unlock premium features. All payment transactions are processed exclusively by Google Play (Android) or Microsoft Store (Windows). We do not receive, process, or store any payment or billing information. Please refer to the privacy policies of Google and Microsoft for details on how they handle transaction data.

Internet Access

The App may use an internet connection solely to verify in-app purchase entitlements through the platform store (Google Play / Microsoft Store). No personal data is transmitted during this process. All technical reference content (thread tables, calculators, engineering data) is stored locally on your device.

Data Deletion & Your Choices

Since the App collects nothing, there is no personal data of yours for us to hold or delete. Anything the App stores — your calculation history, settings and Pro status — lives only on your device, and is erased when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Questions? Email support@hrsoftware.ar.

Third-Party Services

MechRef integrates no analytics, advertising or tracking services. Payment processing is handled by Google Play or the Microsoft Store as described under In-App Purchases — that is the only third party involved.

Children's Privacy

MechRef is a general-audience engineering tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
